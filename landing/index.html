FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need an account to try it?

No. Open the app and use free-tier features without signing up.

Does it work at the venue?

Yes. The app caches for offline use and stores data locally on the device.

How do paid tiers activate?

Paid tiers activate by license key entered in the Upgrade screen. Test checkouts are wired; live checkout and key delivery are being finalized before public launch. Demo keys (BOOTHFLOW-PRO-2026, BOOTHFLOW-BUSINESS-2026) are available for testing.

What's included in Pro?

Unlimited clients, instant quote builder with add-ons, branded galleries with print-order prompts, follow-ups, and the event-day checklist.

What's included in Business?

Everything in Pro, plus multi-operator setup and rebooking prompts based on original event dates.

Where are my photos stored?

Galleries use photo URLs rather than direct uploads in this version. You control where images are hosted and paste links into the gallery.
