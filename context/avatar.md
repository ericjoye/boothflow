# Avatar — Photo Booth Rental Operator

## Who They Are
- **Role**: Owner-operator of a small photo booth rental business
- **Business size**: 1–3 booths, usually 1–2 people, often solo-founder
- **Typical setups**: Enclosed classic booth, 360-degree spin booth, open-air selfie station with ring light + iPad
- **Events**: Weddings, corporate parties, birthdays, bar/bat mitzvahs, holiday parties
- **Geography**: US-based, operates within 60–90 miles of home base; travels to venues

## The Job-To-Be-Done
Turn a wedding lead into a delivered gallery with one smooth workflow — from first text/Instagram DM, to quote, to contract, to event setup, to gallery delivery, to rebooking.

## What They Feel
- **Rush anxiety**: “I need to get this quote back fast or they’ll book someone else.”
- **Chaos dread**: “Where is that client’s deposit? Which add-ons did they choose? Which venue has a 2-hour load-in window?”
- **Gallery shame**: “I still haven’t texted the wedding photos to the bride — it’s been 4 days.”
- **Add-on regret**: “I forgot to offer prints. That was $400 I left on the table.”
- **Solo-founder loneliness**: “I’m running the business, the booth, the editing, and the client comms. I don’t have time to learn complex software.”

## What They Already Use
- Google Calendar for bookings
- Google Docs/Notes for quote templates
- Venmo/Square for deposits and final payment
- Dropbox/Google Drive for gallery storage
- Instagram for marketing and lead generation
- Text/WhatsApp for client communication
- Paper checklist for event-day gear

## What Would Make Them NOT Buy
- A photographer CRM built for portrait/wedding photographers (wrong feature set, too expensive, ignores booth-specific add-ons)
- Enterprise event management platforms (too complex, $99+/mo, built for 20+ person teams)
- A tool that doesn’t include instant quote generation (they need to price in 60 seconds while standing with the client)
- Subscription pricing above $49/mo when they’re doing fewer than 4 events/month
- Something that requires them to “migrate” existing bookings or change how they currently take payments
